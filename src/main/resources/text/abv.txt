ABV is simply an abbreviation for alcohol by volume content. This is simply the percentage of the wine that is alcohol. This is an important filter to consider as there are long term risks associated with overconsumption of alcohol. The New Zealand Nutrition Foundation recommends that you should only drink 2-3 standard drinks in a day (a NZ standard drink is typically 12.5ml which makes this 25-37.5ml a day). Every person should also have at least 2 alcohol-free days a week, meaning you should limit your alcohol consumption to 10-15 standard drinks a week.
